Our mission
Back in the Game · Est. 2025
Founded by Stacy Guevara
Long Island, NY
Back in the Game exists for two kinds of athletes: those who have lost their place in sport — to injury, self-doubt, or rejection — and those who never had a place to begin with.

Stacy Guevara was told to quit soccer in her first year of high school after a long and painful injury recovery that took everything from her. Cycle after cycle of physical therapy. Watching teammates play from the sideline. Instead of giving up, she found purpose in donating gear to children in Central America — and realized that serving others gave her back something the injury had taken: a reason to stay in the game.

Every comeback story shared here earns impact toward getting a child in Central America the equipment they need to play. You recover. You share. A kid somewhere gets to start.
